## My profile

From this section you can update your profile and change your password.

### Profile

Enter your first name, last name, email and emergency contact phone number. These details are visible to platform administrators.

The **emergency phone** field is optional but recommended: it is used by administrators to contact you if needed during a flight.

Click **Save profile** to save your changes.

### Change password

Enter your current password, then your new password (minimum 6 characters) twice. Click **Change password** to confirm.

If you have forgotten your password, contact the administrator.
