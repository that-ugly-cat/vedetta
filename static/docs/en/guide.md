## What is vedetta

vedetta shows the position of pilots in flight on a live map, receiving data from OGN (radio network) and PureTrack (cloud). Telegram notifications alert your team of takeoffs, landings, and lost signals.

### Are you a pilot?

Register, log in, and add your devices (FANET ID, FLARM, PureTrack username…). Join a watchlist from the Watchlist section, then ask the admin to add you to the group's Telegram channel.

### Are you an organizer?

Log in with your admin account, create a watchlist, add pilots and configure the Telegram channel for automatic notifications.

### Supported sources

FANET · FLARM · OGN Tracker · Naviter · ICAO · PureTrack (which includes XContest, Garmin inReach, Syride and others)
