## Watchlist

A **watchlist** is a group of pilots monitored together. Every watchlist **must** have an associated Telegram channel that receives automatic notifications about group events.

### Telegram channel

The **@buco_buco_bot** bot must be present in the watchlist's Telegram channel or group. Without the bot in the channel, no notifications are sent.

If you are in the Telegram channel but not a member of the watchlist, **you will not receive notifications about any pilot in the group**. If you are a member of the watchlist but not in the Telegram channel, others will see notifications about you, but you will not see notifications about anyone.

### How it works

When you are a member of a watchlist, your devices are visible to other members on the live map and included in notifications. The system sends Telegram messages when a pilot in the group takes off, lands, loses signal, and on other events configured by the administrator.

### Joining or leaving

Click **Join** to enter a watchlist, **Leave** to exit. The administrator can also add or remove pilots from the **Admin → Watchlist** section.

Joining a watchlist **does not automatically add you to the Telegram group**. These are two separate things. Once you have joined the watchlist, ask the administrator to also add you to the watchlist's Telegram channel or group.

### Live map

The dropdown at the top left of the map allows you to filter the view to a single watchlist. By default you see all pilots from all your watchlists simultaneously.

### Telegram notifications

Notifications arrive in the Telegram channel or group configured by the administrator.
